The informational page is embedded and linked only within relevant pages, so associates encounter deeper content when they need additional context.

Using progressive disclosure, users were guided with progressively simple answers until they needed to surface answers to more complex solutions.

05 - Learning and Impact

Advocating for designs and aligning strategy with PMs

Prioritizing research-driven findings and communicating solutions to PMs — and understanding why this would benefit associates while supporting business objectives.

Designing across evolving, interconnected experiences

Designing the Help Center alongside an evolving Transfer flow taught me how to maintain consistency across experiences while adapting each solution to serve a distinct purpose.

Designing within and extending established design systems

Working within an existing design system taught me how to make intentional decisions that kept the experience familiar, consistent, and scalable.
