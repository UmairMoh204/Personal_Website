About

Hello! My name is Hamza Mohammed, a UX Designer and Researcher who specializes in enterprise/internal technology. I graduated from Macalester College with a Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science and Cognitive Science with a Psychology minor. At Macalester, I developed technical skills in Python, Java, C, JavaScript, R Language, and SQL. My journey into UX Design began when I asked myself a question for a project I was building in my Computer Science class:

"Why does this Checker's game look and feel very off?"

This would motivate my curiosity in UX Design that lead to my decision to enroll at Prime Digital Academy, an immersion training program preparing individuals for careers in software engineering and UX. This would lead to amazing opportunities to work on projects where I create real user experiences at the City of Saint Paul and Amazon. I’m always exploring new ways to bridge design and technology to create experiences that are not only functional but genuinely meaningful.

UX journey

01 · Macalester College (As a Posse Scholar)

As a first-generation Posse Scholar I learned to find my footing in college and to voice my position in global affairs.

02 · Prime Digital Academy

Prime introduced me to UX principles and methods, and taught me the full process from research through design.

03 · Human-Computer Interaction

HCI at Macalester put people next to the system — research spine under the craft Prime taught.

04 · City of Saint Paul

Saint Paul was my first role as a user researcher. I learned civic tools, how to work a site in a refresh, and how to finish the first phase of a UX process with a clean handoff.

05 · Amazon

At Amazon I designed inside a real org, using a functional design system with real constraints — internal experiences for associates.
