About Me

Hi! I’m Hamza Mohammed, a first-generation college student at Macalester College studying Computer Science with a concentration in Cognitive Science. I’m a Posse Foundation Leadership Scholar and a UNCF/3M Scholar.

I discovered a passion for understanding people and technology early on, which led me to UX/UI Design. I earned a full-ride certification with Prime Digital Academy, interned at the City of Saint Paul as a UX Designer and Researcher, and later at Amazon. I continue to develop my skills by integrating UX Design with software development in my coursework.

When I’m not designing, I enjoy exploring how technology and human behavior intersect, solving problems, and refining my approach to user-centered design.
