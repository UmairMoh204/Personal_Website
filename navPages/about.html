Hamza Mohammed 01 · College, Posse, and NSBE

Going to college — and learning how to lead

I arrived at Macalester as a first-generation student and a Posse Foundation Leadership Scholar. Posse was not just a scholarship. It was the first place I had to practice leading a cohort — showing up for other people, not only for my own classes.

That work continued as Treasurer of NSBE. I learned how a student organization actually stays standing: budgets, follow-through, and making space for other Black engineers on campus.

Hamza Mohammed 02 · Prime Digital Academy

Becoming a UX designer

Prime Digital Academy is where the title became real. I earned a full-ride certification and learned the craft in sequence — research, flows, critique, and shipping a product someone else could use.

This is the chapter where I stopped treating design as a side interest and started treating it as the work.

Hamza Mohammed 03 · Human-Computer Interaction · Macalester

HCI — putting people next to the system

The Human-Computer Interaction course at Macalester is where Computer Science and Cognitive Science met on the same desk. I was no longer only writing code or only thinking about users. I was asking how a person actually holds a system.

Hamza Mohammed 04 · Internship · Amazon

Designing help for people already at work

At Amazon I interned as a UX designer on Workforce Solutions Transformative Tech. The work was an internal help destination for associates in transfer — using inquiry data so people could finish a process without getting stuck.

This is the chapter where school methods had to survive a real org, a real design system, and real constraints.
